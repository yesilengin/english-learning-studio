The Lost Cat

Sarah lives in a small town. She is twelve years old. Sarah has a cat named Lucy. Lucy is white with black spots.

One morning, Sarah wakes up. She looks for Lucy. Lucy is not in her bed. Lucy is not in the kitchen. Sarah is worried.

"Mom, I cannot find Lucy!" Sarah says.

"Don't worry," says Mom. "Let's look outside."

Sarah and her mom go outside. They walk around the house. They call Lucy's name. "Lucy! Lucy!" they shout. But Lucy does not come.

Sarah walks to the neighbor's house. Mrs. Brown lives there. Mrs. Brown is old and kind.

"Hello, Sarah," says Mrs. Brown. "What's wrong?"

"I lost my cat," Sarah says. "Her name is Lucy. She is white with black spots."

"I saw a cat this morning," says Mrs. Brown. "She was near the big tree in my backyard."

Sarah runs to the backyard. She sees the big tree. She looks up. Lucy is sitting on a high branch!

"Lucy!" Sarah calls. Lucy meows loudly. But Lucy is scared. She cannot come down.

Sarah thinks. What can she do? She remembers something. Lucy loves fish. Sarah runs home. She gets a can of fish from the kitchen.

Sarah goes back to the tree. She opens the can. The smell is strong. Lucy smells the fish. Lucy climbs down slowly. She jumps to the ground.

Sarah picks up Lucy. "I was so worried!" Sarah says. Lucy purrs and rubs against Sarah.

"Thank you, Mrs. Brown!" Sarah says.

"You're welcome," says Mrs. Brown. "I'm happy you found her."

Sarah carries Lucy home. She gives Lucy the fish. Lucy eats and eats. Sarah pets Lucy softly.

"Please don't run away again," Sarah whispers.

Lucy meows. It sounds like "I promise."

Sarah smiles. She is happy. Lucy is safe at home.

The end.
